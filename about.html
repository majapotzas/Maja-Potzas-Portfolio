Meet the grocer

Hi, I'm Maja

I help build fun, creative, bold and exciting brand identities, from logos and packaging to posters, social content and the websites that bring them to life.

Who am I?

I’m Maja, and I love colour, as you can probably tell! Nature is my biggest inspiration, but I find ideas everywhere: in different cultures, in people, in everyday life. I want my brands to work the same way, built around community, so they feel like something you’re part of, not just something you buy. That’s what I’d love to do for your business.

Where do I come from?

I grew up in Zimbabwe, surrounded by colour, texture and life, and I’ve carried that with me ever since. I’ve travelled a lot too, and I always seem to bring something back from each place that ends up in my work.
