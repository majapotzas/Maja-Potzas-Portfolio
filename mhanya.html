Mhanya

Mhanya: a fictional running club

Mhanya means “run” back home in Zimbabwe, and that's the whole idea: put your shoes on and go.

That doesn't make it a beginners' club. You can still train hard and push yourself. You just don't need a watch, a 12-week plan and expensive trainers before you're allowed out the door. Mhanya is sweaty, colourful and a bit rough around the edges. It's about showing up and just running.

That's where the Run Box comes in. Every meeting point, whether it's the hill for sprints, the lake for the long run, or a spot in town, has lockers where you can leave your phone, keys and anything else you don't want to carry. No excuses, just run.
