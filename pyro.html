Pyro

PYRO: a fictional coffee roastery and ceramics studio

PYRO is a place to learn about your coffee and make the mug you drink it from. There's no snobbery and no judgement if you don't know your flat white from your cortado.

With ceramics classes, a build-your-own blend bar and Friday night discos, it's somewhere to come with your mates and keep coming back.

The launch starts with someone spilt the beans: teaser posters and a bean hunt around town, with a month of free coffee for whoever finds them. Then Mug Shots takes over, with free classes for anyone who leaves their mug behind.
