Mangi

Mangi: a self-initiated rebrand of a real Italian restaurant in Jönköping

Mangi already had the personality. The food is loud, warm and Italian, and so are the people behind it. The branding just wasn't showing it.

The new identity lets that come through. Walking in should feel like stepping into an honest kitchen: big flavours, a good atmosphere, and family at the table. It's an affordable place where locals come back again and again for good food. The colour palette comes straight from the staples of an Italian pantry: olive oil, tomatoes, and cacao for the tiramisu.

The base was there. It just needed some flavour.
